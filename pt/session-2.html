    Usability and User Experience Assessment of an Agricultural Application for Coffee Farming
    Luiz Rezende (Universidade Federal de Itajubá), Rodrigo Seabra (Universidade Federal de Itajubá), Vanessa Souza (Universidade Federal de Itajubá), Margarete Volpato (Empresa de Pesquisa Agropecuária de Minas Gerais), Vania Silva (Empresa de Pesquisa Agropecuária de Minas Gerais), Helena Alves (Empresa Brasileira de Pesquisa Agropecuária)
    Resumo

    Driven by Agriculture 4.0, the digital transformation in agribusiness has made mobile devices essential tools in rural management. However, the effectiveness of these technologies directly depends on the quality of the interaction between the user and the interface. This work presents an evaluation of the usability and user experience (UX) of the Regador application, a decision-support system for monitoring water potential in coffee farming in the State of Minas Gerais based on remote sensing data. The methodology adopted was a case study conducted with eight volunteer participants, including producers, technicians, and researchers. Usability tests were applied with the Think Aloud protocol, a profile questionnaire, and the System Usability Scale (SUS). The results revealed that although the application has the potential to support agricultural activities, there are opportunities to improve the registration flow, navigation, and the visibility of certain commands. These findings reinforce the importance of conducting usability assessments for applications aimed at the agricultural sector, especially given the diversity of user profiles.
